Championship Manager 97/98

For iPhone and iPad. Bring your own copy.

First, add it to your Home Screen

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open CM 97/98 from the new icon. Load your game there: the Home Screen app keeps its own storage, works offline, and runs full screen.

How to get your game onto the phone
  1. Put your CM 97/98 folder (the one with CM2E16.EXE in it) in the Files app, in iCloud Drive or On My iPhone.
  2. Press and hold the folder, then tap Compress. That makes a .zip.
  3. Come back here, tap Load game and pick the .zip.

The game is stored only on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Saves are kept on this device automatically after you save in the game. A backup puts a copy in Files.

Playing

  1. Landscape is best. Tap to click.
  2. Pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to move around. Fit puts it back.
  3. Keys opens the keyboard for names and searches.
  4. Save in the game as usual, then Exit to come back here.

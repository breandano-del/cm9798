    2.

    3.

Playing

      
    
        
  1. Landscape is best. Tap to click. Drag with one finger to drag (players, arrows on the tactics screen).
    2. 
        
  2. Press and hold for a right click. Right makes your next tap or drag use the right button.
    3. 
        
  3. Scroll with two fingers, pinch to zoom. Wide fills the screen width (bigger text), Fit shows the whole screen.
    4. 
        
  4. Keys opens the keyboard for names and searches.
    5. 
        
  5. Save in the game as usual, then Exit to come back here.
